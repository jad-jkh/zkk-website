About

About ZKK Consulting LLC

ZKK Consulting LLC is a two-person Texas consulting firm for engineering-led products. It provides fixed-price market research, patent landscape research, competitive pricing analysis and strategy research for startups and medical device teams.

Who is ZKK Consulting?

ZKK Consulting is a two-person firm. You work directly with the people doing the research, without an account layer between you and the work. Both founders are engineers.

What

What does ZKK Consulting do?

ZKK researches the market, the competition, and the landscape around a decision, then hands you the organized analysis behind it. Most engagements combine several of the four areas below.

  1. 01

    Market & competitive intelligence

    Research on the market, competitors, offerings, pricing, positioning, and potential white space.

  2. 02

    Market sizing & growth analysis

    Market sizing, including TAM, SAM, and SOM, grounded in traceable sources.

  3. 03

    Specialized & regulatory research

    Technical and specialized research across patents, regulations, standards, certifications, supply chains, and components. Research and analysis, not legal advice.

  4. 04

    Investor & decision materials

    Clear, well-sourced materials for a fundraise, board discussion, or internal decision.

Full scope and prices are on the services and pricing page. Deeper technical work is described on the specialized expertise page.

How it works

How does an engagement run?

An engagement runs in four steps, from a no-cost discovery call to a delivered, sourced report. Timelines are set during scoping based on the scope of the work and your deadlines.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    A no-cost conversation about your business, market, and the decision the research should inform.

  2. 02

    Scope & agreement

    We confirm deliverables, selected add-ons, price, and timeline, then send a Statement of Work.

  3. 03

    Research

    Market, competitive, technical, and regulatory research, with sources and assumptions documented throughout.

  4. 04

    Delivery & walkthrough

    A finished, sourced deliverable you can circulate, followed by a live walkthrough of the findings and your questions.

A 50% deposit starts the work and the remaining 50% is invoiced on completion. One round of revisions is included. We can sign a mutual NDA before you share proprietary information.

Scope noteWe provide research and analysis, including sourced findings, assumptions, estimates, and decision-relevant context. We do not provide legal, regulatory, medical, tax, accounting, or investment advice, and our deliverables are not a substitute for those services.
