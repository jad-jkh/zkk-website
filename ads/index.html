About these films

ZKK Consulting makes short films to show how research decisions get made, and where they go wrong. The Typical Research Report shows what a standard consulting deliverable looks like and why decision-makers rarely finish reading it. What Everyone Missed in The Wolf Of Wall Street is an audio-led explainer that looks at a famous film through a market-research lens.

Both films are free to watch and share. To see the kind of work behind them, read about our research services or browse a sample report.
