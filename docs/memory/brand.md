# Brand (from the ZKK report spec v22 and template)

Palette: band green #133423, deep green #013220, mid green #0B4A32, gold #B8860B (rules and large type only), light gold #E5B93C (text on green), gold ink #8A6508 (gold text on cream/white), brown #7A5523, cream #F3ECDF, cream text #FBF9F3, table band #F7F2E8, rule #CDBFA3, light rule #E3DCCB, ink #1A1A1A, muted #5F6F66, grey #6B6B6B, callout #F2F2F2.

Fonts: Cormorant Garamond for headings (bold; hero title regular), Inter for everything else. Both OFL, self-host WOFF2. Lining figures in headings, tabular figures in tables.

Logo: serif "ZKK" in #013220 with a gold diamond and gold small-caps CONSULTING. Green on cream/white, white on green. No effects.

Rules: no shadows, gradients, glass, radius, stock photos, AI art, emoji or decorative icons. One accent per chart. Contrast: never use #B8860B for text under 24 px.

Voice: plain, direct, answer first, limits stated next to claims, dates on everything. No em or en dashes.
