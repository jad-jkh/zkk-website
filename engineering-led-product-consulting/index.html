Service

Engineering-led product consulting

Engineering-led product consulting is market, patent, strategic and competitive pricing work done by engineers for teams building technical products. Packages have fixed base prices from $999, and scope is agreed before work starts.

What it is

Engineering context for commercial questions

Both founders are engineers. That background helps us interpret technical documentation, understand product claims in context, and connect them to market and commercial research.

  • We work from the primary sources. Datasheets, standards documents, regulatory databases, patents, and published literature are core research inputs, not material handed off for someone else to interpret.
  • We understand technical feasibility in context. Engineering training helps us recognize when a product claim is technically demanding, physically constrained, or economically difficult to scale.
  • We turn research into usable visuals. Product renders, technical illustrations, exploded views, system diagrams, and short explainer animations are produced in-house.
