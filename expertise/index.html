Engineering

3. Engineering context for commercial questions.

Both founders are engineers. That background helps us interpret technical documentation, understand product claims in context, and connect them to market and commercial research.

We work from the primary sources

Datasheets, standards documents, regulatory databases, patents, and published literature are core research inputs, not material handed off for someone else to interpret.

We understand technical feasibility in context

Engineering training helps us recognize when a product claim is technically demanding, physically constrained, or economically difficult to scale.

We turn research into usable visuals

Product renders, technical illustrations, exploded views, system diagrams, and short explainer animations are produced in-house. The same engagement can deliver both the research and the visuals that communicate it.

Visual communication

When a picture does the work

Investor decks, grant applications, technical documentation, conference posters, and internal reviews are clearer when research is translated into visuals. Visual services can be added to an engagement or commissioned separately.

  • Product renders and concept visualizations
  • Technical illustrations and exploded views
  • System and process diagrams
  • Short explainer animations and motion graphics
  • Pitch deck figures, charts, and slide design
  • Document formatting and visual redesign
