What We Do

Common questions we help answer

Most engagements combine several of these areas. Each is tailored to the industry, audience, and question at hand.

Market & competitive intelligence

Research on the market, competitors, offerings, pricing, positioning, and potential white space. Includes competitive landscapes, competitor matrices, and buyer and channel research.

Market sizing & growth analysis

Market sizing, including TAM, SAM, and SOM, grounded in traceable sources. Also includes demand drivers, adjacent-market analysis, and multi-year revenue and cost models in editable Excel.

Specialized & regulatory research

Technical and specialized research across patents, regulations, standards, certifications, supply chains, and components. Research and analysis, not legal advice.

Investor & decision materials

Clear, well-sourced materials for a fundraise, board discussion, or internal decision: market analysis, pricing and go-to-market research, pitch-deck review or development, and decision-ready visuals.
