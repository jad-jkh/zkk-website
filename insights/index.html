How the research works

  • How to build a competitor analysis for your pitch deck13 October 2026

    A competitor slide has about a minute of an investor's attention. This guide covers how to choose who goes on it, what to compare, and how to back it up.

  • Research consultant vs doing it yourself or with AI tools13 October 2026

    AI tools have made it much easier to get a first draft of market research. The harder question is when a draft is enough and when it is worth paying someone to do the work.

  • How to do a patent landscape search13 October 2026

    A patent landscape organizes public patent information around a technology so a team can see who is filing, where activity concentrates, and what the claims cover. This guide shows how one is put together and where its limits are.

  • How to size a market for a new device13 October 2026

    A step-by-step method for hardware and device startups who need a market size they can defend, built from sourced inputs rather than a single large number.

  • Patent landscape vs freedom to operate: what each one is13 October 2026

    A patent landscape and a freedom-to-operate analysis are often mixed up. They answer different questions and are done by different people.

  • TAM, SAM, and SOM for medical devices13 October 2026

    Three numbers show up in almost every device pitch deck. This guide explains what each one means, how to build them, and what an investor will ask when they look closely.
