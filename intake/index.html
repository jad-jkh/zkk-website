What happens next
  1. Within one business day: We review your request and reply to confirm whether it is a fit.
  2. Discovery call: We discuss your business, market, and the decision the research needs to support.
  3. Scope and agreement: If we move forward, we send a Statement of Work covering the scope, deliverables, price, and proposed timeline.
  4. Delivery: We provide the agreed deliverables, walk through the findings, and invoice the remaining 50% on completion.
How payment works

Nothing is payable through this form. If we agree to move forward, we send a Statement of Work confirming the scope, deliverables, timeline, and price. After it is signed, we send a Stripe invoice for the 50% deposit. Work begins once we have the signed SOW, the deposit, and the materials needed to begin. The remaining 50% is invoiced on completion, after the agreed deliverable is provided.

Invoices are processed securely through Stripe. ZKK Consulting does not receive or store your card details.

Do not make a payment before the scope is agreed and the SOW is signed. If you receive a payment request from any address other than info@zkkconsulting.com, zolomon@zkkconsulting.com, or jad@zkkconsulting.com, treat it as suspicious and email us directly. Payment, cancellation, and refund terms are set out in the Statement of Work.
