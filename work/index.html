Independent sample · Medical devices

Market, patent, and commercial research for wrist-worn fall detection

Comprehensive package sample + Patent Landscape Research

Question. An internal hardware program needs a grounded view of its United States market, competitive and patent context, and the commercial assumptions behind a five-year outlook.

Research. Market sizing, competitor, buyer, patent, regulatory and reimbursement, supply-chain, pricing, and channel research, paired with an editable five-year commercial model.

Result. A source-supported report and companion Excel model that separate sourced inputs from unmeasured commercial drivers and make base, downside, and upside cases clear. This reflects a Comprehensive engagement with Patent Landscape Research. Independent sample created from ZKK's own internal hardware program. It is not client work. Public research is real and sourced; model projections are illustrative, based on stated assumptions, and are not forecasts.

Independent sample · Medical devices

Competitive landscape for a continuous monitoring device

Launchpad package sample

Question. A hypothetical early-stage company entering a crowded monitoring category needs a clear view of existing competitors, their positioning, and the gaps or distinctions relevant to a product decision.

Research. Competitive landscape research mapping the field, profiling relevant competitors by product and positioning, and documenting demand context with traceable sources.

Result. A concise, circulable document that gives a founding team a clear view of the field and the assumptions that warrant further testing. This reflects the deliverable format of a Launchpad engagement.
